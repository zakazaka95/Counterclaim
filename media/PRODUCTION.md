# Counterclaim — Partner Demo Lab film

## Brief and factual boundary

An independent working demonstration of Internet Court + GenLayer + Yellow.
The viewer should remember: **disputed work can become an agreed session outcome**.
Audience: partner teams and builders. One CTA: reproduce the demo and inspect its code.
Delivery: 42 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps H.264/AAC MP4; adapt portrait if feasible.

The demonstration uses a real signed Yellow sandbox session with zero asset
allocations, and an actual GenLayer decision over three frozen technical milestones
(weights 40/35/25). The 100 credits are explicitly nonmonetary demo units. There
must be no dollar, USDC, payout or automatic-enforcement claim. Both sandbox agents
must sign the proposed verdict-bound update/closure. The final film can show success
only after those exact facts exist in the saved public proof. Rendering a styleframe
is not evidence that the integration works.

## Chosen visual grammar

Editorial court docket meets Yellow settlement rail. Warm paper #F5F2EA, ink
#070707, official Yellow #FCD000, Internet Court red preserved only in its official
mark/status context. Instrument Serif for one large statement; DM Sans for practical
captions and data. Official marks are static, undistorted and isolated with clear
space. No neon fog, coin trading graphics, game HUD or decorative dashboards.

The recurring object is one case document. Its evidence becomes a verdict, then the
same verdict digest enters the signed Yellow session record. Camera movement follows
this causal path. Information-heavy screenshots stay front-facing. One brief
perspective establishing move is permitted; readable UI is then flat and sharp.
Movement uses strong deceleration, 18–28 frame transitions and real holds, without
bounce on financial-looking data or logos.

## Assets, sound and review

Official source URLs and hashes are in `ASSET-PROVENANCE.json`. Fonts include their
SIL OFL licenses. Yellow's downloaded SDK graphic is reference-only unless a shot
needs it; unrelated generic technology art is excluded from the film.

Use an original restrained electronic/percussive score and tactile transitions,
composed on this film's exact 42-second clock. Avoid uncleared historical template
audio. Keep music and SFX stems separate; retain an SFX-only export if useful.

Before export, ingest genuine proof and capture actual UI views. Inspect a styleframe
at full and phone size, each scene and transition neighbors, shuffled seeks, full
encoded frame continuity, duration and audio peaks/loudness. Obtain independent
critique from a teammate. Do not call controlled local logic tests live validator
benchmarks. Do not claim playback/listening checks from stills or codec metadata.
